Shuo Yan

I am an undergraduate student in Health Science and Technology at the Global Institute of Future Technology, Shanghai Jiao Tong University. I am currently working with Prof. Shaobo Cui at DeepDelta Lab.

My research interests center on memory mechanisms in AI agents, particularly memory representation, organization, and adaptive updating. I am exploring how these mechanisms support long-term reasoning in streaming video understanding. I am also interested in blockchain technologies and decentralized applications.
